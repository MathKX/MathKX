## What does x + 3 = 7 mean? {#meaning}

An **equation** has an equals sign between two sides. The equals sign means that both sides have **the same value**.

**x** is a number we don’t know yet. The equation $x + 3 = 7$ says: “A number plus 3 is the same as 7.” Solving the equation means finding which number x is.

## Think of a balance {#balance}

Imagine a pair of scales that is level. On the left there is a box holding x ones, and three loose ones. On the right there are seven ones. The scales are level, so both sides weigh the same.

If you remove the same amount from both sides, the scales stay level. Go through the steps:

## Do the same to both sides {#both-sides}

We want x on its own. The left side has $+ 3$, so we subtract 3, and we do it on **both** sides:

$$x + 3 − 3 = 7 − 3$$

$$x = 4$$

## Check the answer {#check}

Put your answer back into the original equation. If both sides are equal, the answer is correct:

$$4 + 3 = 7$$

:::example{title="Example: x + 5 = 9" id="example"}
1. Subtract 5 from both sides: $x + 5 − 5 = 9 − 5$.
2. Now x is on its own: $x = 4$.
3. Check: $4 + 5 = 9$. It works.
:::

## A common mistake {#mistake}

Some people add 3 instead of subtracting, and get $x = 10$. The check reveals it: $10 + 3 = 13$, not 7. Substituting your answer is a good habit, however you solved the equation.
