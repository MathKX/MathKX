# Design review decision log (rounds 01–03)

Maintained per [review-process.md](../review-process.md) §3. Rows record owner decisions explicitly; anything not marked as an owner decision is pending. Reviewer agreement is not approval. "Implementation guidance" means the row records a correction that reviewers agree on and that needs no owner decision; the owner may still overrule it.

## Round-03 prototype findings

Round-03 findings (Claude 1–12, Codex R03-01 to 08, Gemini P0–P2) are prototype defects, not planning decisions. The prototype's [review response](../../../Prototype/mathkx-designsystemet/docs/iteration-2/REVIEW-RESPONSE.md) reports each as fixed, not changed, or corrected, with domain and browser checks. They are not logged individually here; the rules they establish are carried into the addendum's §E, and the four behaviour decisions they raised are DL-026. Independent verification at baseline `9162640`: prototype `npm test` 16 of 16, `npm run build` success; the browser suite and device checks were not rerun by the log's author.
