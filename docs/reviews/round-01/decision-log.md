# Design review decision log (rounds 01–04)

Maintained per [review-process.md](../review-process.md) §3. Rows record owner decisions explicitly; anything not marked as an owner decision is pending. Reviewer agreement is not approval. "Implementation guidance" means the row records a correction that reviewers agree on and that needs no owner decision; the owner may still overrule it.

Owner decisions recorded on 2026-10-18 were given in conversation with the owner (Rune T. Larsen) after the round-04 verification, synthesis, and challenge; each row's status cell says so. Reconciliation of approved decisions into the authoritative documents is the consolidation step of review-process §5 and has not yet been performed unless a row says otherwise.
